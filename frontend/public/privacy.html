Privacy policy

This site is a demo of a text-and-voice onboarding assistant, built as a take-home project. It is not a commercial product.

  • What is stored: the messages you send, transcripts of voice calls, voice messages you record, and the details the assistant learns (the names you give it and what you want help with). These are stored per phone number you enter, on the demo server.
  • Google data: if you connect a Google account, the assistant can read and search your Gmail, draft emails, and read and add calendar events. It sends email or creates events only after you say yes. The access token is stored encrypted and is used only to answer your requests in this demo. Google data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.
  • AI processing: messages, audio and the Google data needed to answer a request are sent to OpenAI to generate replies and speech.
  • Deleting your data: resetting the conversation in the app disconnects Google, which revokes the token. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
